# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Connected and isolated screenshot canvases with drag-and-drop captures.
- Official device frames for iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo, Apple Watch, Apple TV,
  CarPlay, Mac, Android phones and tablets, Google Play feature graphic and the
  App Store universal, header and search-results creatives.
- Apple's required export sizes, with one ZIP per export and a folder per
  support and language.
- Multi-support export: select the decks you have worked on and bundle them in
  one ZIP.
- Automatic translation into the five App Store languages.
- Themes, palettes, gradients, textures, scenes, typography and imported fonts.
- Style Lab, cross-device adaptation, saved looks and image overlays.
- English interface by default, with Spanish available from the toolbar.
- Local project storage, native macOS menus and an in-app welcome guide.
- Signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple.
